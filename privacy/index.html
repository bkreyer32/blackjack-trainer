Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Blackjack Counter (“the app”) is a blackjack and card-counting practice tool. It does not collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of its own. We never receive your name, email, location, device identifiers, or usage information.

What stays on your device

Your table rules, settings and practice statistics are stored only on your device (in the app’s local storage). They are never uploaded. If you use Export backup, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

The optional “Unlock Counting & Deviations” purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details or Apple ID. The app only asks the App Store whether this Apple ID owns the unlock.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

The web version

The web version of the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub may process standard request logs (such as your IP address) when your browser loads the pages. We do not have access to those logs. The web version stores its data in your browser only.

Children

The app is a simulation with no real-money gambling. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It is intended for adults interested in learning blackjack strategy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
