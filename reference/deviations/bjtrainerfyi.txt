 Reading this chart
 What does a two-tone cell mean?
 Both sides of one index. The lower-left color is the basic-strategy play while the true count is below the index; the corner color is the play once the count reaches it. The number in the cell is the index itself.
 What game is this chart for?
 The common 6-deck game: dealer hits soft 17, double after split allowed, late surrender offered — with the S17 column showing where standing on soft 17 moves an index. For any other rule set, the interactive chart regenerates every cell for the exact table you play.
 Why might another site's chart show slightly different numbers?
 Indices depend on the rules and the rounding conventions used when they were computed, so published tables differ by a point here and there. These are the standard Hi-Lo values, and they're rendered from the same engine that grades your play in the trainer — the chart and your practice feedback always agree.
 How to actually learn these
 Recalling the right index mid-shoe is the hard part, not reading the chart. The trainer keeps a live Hi-Lo count and grades every decision against this chart as you play, and the interactive chart can preview any true count to show exactly which cells flip.
