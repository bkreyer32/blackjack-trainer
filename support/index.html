Restore a purchase

In the app, open Settings (⚙︎) → Unlock → Restore Purchases. You must be signed in with the same Apple ID that bought the unlock. Offer codes can be redeemed from the same place with Redeem Code.

Back up or move your stats

Open Settings → Your data → Export backup to save a file, and Import backup on the other device. Backups contain your rules, settings and statistics, never your purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app use real money? No. All play is simulated with imaginary units. There are no prizes.

Is card counting legal? Yes, but casinos are private property and may ask you to stop playing or leave.

Do you collect my data? No. See the Privacy Policy.

Which strategy charts are used? Basic strategy is computed for the rules you choose. Hi-Lo index plays follow Don Schlesinger’s Illustrious 18 and Fab 4 (Blackjack Attack), with separate H17 and S17 tables.

The app says an index play I know differs. Published index tables differ by a point here and there depending on rules and rounding.
